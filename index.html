Inkhaven 3

A 30-day writing residency at Lighthaven, Berkeley.

Why apply?

Being here can put you close to the world-story. We met Grimes here, for instance.

You will get to be with the most open and intelligent people in Silicon Valley for a month. You will get to springboard yourself into the SV intellectual scene. And, above all, you will get to understand and practice demonic warfare in a relatively controlled environment.

This could be your hyperbolic time-chamber for depossession.
