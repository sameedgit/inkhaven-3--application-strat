Guidebook

Deliverables Needed

These are the things needed to complete the application. Each corresponds to a question on the form, and each has a rate to keep up.

1 · Platform accounts

Where else your writing lives — X, Substack, a LessWrong profile. A visible track record of publishing is clearly preferred.

Pen-names are allowed. A pseudonym is best for maximal dynamicity.

KPI
  • Make a Substack, and/or an account on LessWrong.
  • Settle the name first, real or pen — everything downstream inherits it.
  • Fill them out properly: handle, bio, avatar, about page. A blank account reads worse than no account.

2 · The daily posts

Volume, published in public, to build an account that looks alive. Quality matters far less than the streak. Post to LessWrong where you can — it is the only platform where the track record carries a visible number.

KPI
  • One post a day, 500+ words. More in the action plan.
  • Aim for at least one "discourse-generating" effort-post — something that actually starts an argument.
  • Hit the karma target off one of them.

3 · The "why"

Why you are applying, and what you hope happens to you. A rationale for: why the programme suits you, and why you suit it.

It must hold logically while carrying a sense of purpose: that grander register belongs there, but must not take over.

KPI
  • Under 500 words.
  • Name a real opportunity cost, and take a position on why writing matters in 2026. They ask for both; most applicants give neither.
  • Can wait — write it near the end.

4 · The references

Anyone who can vouch for you as a thinker or writer on abilities that the application itself can't showcase. We are already targeting these people, so a reference can follow from the relationship.

A reference is downstream of the reach-out, so it cannot be crammed — start on day one and keep going. The list of 133 is here.

KPI
  • Two reach-outs a day — follow everyone on X, comment and reply over the month, then ask.
  • Reference asked of everyone warm by week three.

5 · The essays — up to five

Stated on the form as the primary thing you will be evaluated on. Up to five, and these are what you actually submit.

What matters here is up to five — preferably at least three — essays on optimally selected topics, with a single theme or thesis running through them. High quality, 1,000–1,500 words, one lane each.

For help with topic selection, refer to the writing advice.

KPI
  • One essay a week.
  • Read by at least two of us before it goes up.
  • Pick a lane off the heat map and settle the through-line before writing to it.
  • Hit the karma target off one of them.
